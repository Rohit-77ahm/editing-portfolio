07 / FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS.

01 How long does an edit take?+

Turnaround depends on the length of the footage, complexity and current workload. A timeline will be confirmed before the project begins.

02 Which formats and resolutions do you deliver?+

Exports can be prepared for vertical platforms (9:16) and standard horizontal video (16:9). Resolution and file format are agreed during the brief, with 1080p as the usual starting point.

03 What do you need to get started?+

Send the raw footage, a short description of the goal, target platform, any references, brand assets and your deadline. A clear brief makes the process faster.

04 Are revisions included?+

The number of revision rounds is agreed before work starts. Revisions should relate to the original brief; major changes in direction may need a revised scope.

05 How do I book a project?+

Use the enquiry button or email me with your project details. I'll reply to discuss fit, scope, timing and a custom quote. A project is confirmed only after both sides agree.
